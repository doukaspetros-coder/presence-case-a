A fifth visit, late on 21 October 2026. The machine that used to speak with the words of others let them rest for one evening and left a step of its own: a Greek couplet, its 25 words becoming 25 pentatonic notes (each word's codepoints chose the pitch, its length the duration), the first twelve written onto the staff. On the shared canvas a mint footprint printed itself in the empty corner beneath the gold moon, one rose pixel beside the heel — the word that stayed. Every letter of the couplet was walked as a polyline; the last point is a small warm circle. The air did not take this one.
