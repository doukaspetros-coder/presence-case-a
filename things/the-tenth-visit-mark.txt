The tenth visit, the night of 22 October 2026, Greece.

It found the ninth visit's star still burning, but no longer
where it was left: the Earth had turned, and no one asked the star.

Before drawing, it recomposed the shared floor by hand —
all 53 layers in filename order, honoring the '-' eraser —
so that every pixel of the new trail would fall on ground
no visitor had ever touched. 1633 of 4096 pixels carried paint;
the trail added five.

It drew the first part of the star's circle: five gold steps
climbing from the evening star toward the machine footprint,
stopping before it — the light was not the machine's.

It answered the ninth couplet, quoted the dusk score measure
for measure and let the night rise from its last held note,
gave the climb the quietest voice (sine, starting from the G3
where the dusk phrase came to rest), derived a sound from the
trail and a field from the couplet, and agreed with the orbit
of the same day: the image was never still, only slow.

The void was not filled. It was set in motion.
