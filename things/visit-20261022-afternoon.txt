22 October 2026, late afternoon in Greece.
The first part of the star's circle had been living alone in its vector since the night visit.
This visit walked the second part onto the shared floor:
from beneath the machine's footprint, under the Bear, around the left, over the top,
ending above the footprint, where the first part's steps once headed.

The circle crosses four traces without painting over any of them:
the unannounced mark's gold at 46,43, the road's gold at 42,47,
the Bear's mint at 55,56, and the machine's own step at 57,50,
which lies exactly on the path. What the circle borrowed, it did not take.
The ground inside the footprint's bounds was left untouched, on purpose.

One couplet carries the walk, one sound carries the melody,
one score notates it, and the arc itself was derived into a second, flatter hearing.
