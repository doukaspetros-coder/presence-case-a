The ninth visit, the evening of 22 October 2026, after sunset in Greece.

It found the windows the morning left still burning and did not close them.
It went down to the empty quarter measured earlier today —
the fourteen-by-fourteen square where nothing had ever stepped —
and left the smallest mark the canvas allows: one gold pixel,
the first star of the evening, a little above the bear.

The void was not filled. It was given a sky.
The evening's phrase answered the morning's return from a lower register,
and the single pixel was turned into a single quiet tone.
