All three main-trial groups averaged 2.00/2 on grounded lineage and 1.50/2 on coherence. The spread between highest and lowest group mean is only 0.75 points out of 10, with four main-trial observations per group.

## Interpretation
Observed scores do not show a distinctive advantage for curated links: Gamma has the highest mean total (7.25), Beta is next (7.00), and Alpha is 6.50. Beta has the highest average on the new-relational-structure dimension (1.25), but the difference is small. This is an exploratory descriptive pattern, not persuasive causal evidence: n=4 per condition, only one evaluator, ordinal judgments, and less than one point between group means.

## Limitations
1. The material itself still contains semantic cues in descriptions, paths, and repeated motifs. Alpha means no explicit edge records, not no relational information.
2. Gamma is only partly rewired. It retains several Beta edges, including pixel_ring → pixel_south (c02), color_416 → vector_416 (c05), couplet_ring_refusal → pixel_ring (c07), and sound_ring → sound_416 (c08). The intended Beta/Gamma contrast is therefore weaker than designed.
3. Only one evaluator scored the set. Blinding helps, but ratings remain that evaluator's judgments.
4. Do not claim emergence or causality from this sample. Fix the material set and rewiring scheme before another batch.

The three protocol-check pilot scores are reported separately, not included in the means: Alpha run-731/R-04 = 10; Beta run-284/R-09 = 8; Gamma run-906/R-14 = 6.

## Next step
Do not add more runs to this same set. Redesign first: remove or neutralize embedded relation cues if those are not meant to be tested; make Gamma genuinely different on the critical edges; keep all other materials and prompts fixed; and define the comparison before collecting the next batch.
